## Bruschetta

### Description
A simple Italian appetizer of toasted bread topped with fresh tomatoes, garlic, basil, and olive oil.

### Ingredients
- 1 baguette or Italian bread loaf
- 4 ripe tomatoes, diced
- 2 cloves garlic (1 whole, 1 minced)
- 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Preparation
1. Slice the bread into 1/2-inch thick pieces and toast until golden.
2. Rub each toasted slice with the whole garlic clove.
3. In a bowl, combine diced tomatoes, minced garlic, basil, olive oil, and balsamic vinegar.
4. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Spoon the tomato mixture onto the toasted bread just before serving.

## Chocolate Cake

### Description
A rich, moist chocolate cake perfect for celebrations or everyday indulgence.

### Ingredients
- 1 3/4 cups flour
- 2 cups sugar
- 3/4 cup cocoa powder
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup boiling water

### Preparation
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease two 9-inch round pans.
2. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
3. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla; beat on medium speed for 2 minutes.
4. Stir in the boiling water (the batter will be thin).
5. Pour batter evenly into the pans.
6. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Cool in pans for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.

## Chocolate Mousse

### Description
A light, airy dessert made from whipped cream and melted chocolate for a smooth, decadent texture.

### Ingredients
- 200g dark chocolate, chopped
- 3 eggs, separated
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

### Preparation
1. Melt the chocolate over a double boiler; let cool slightly.
2. Whisk egg yolks with half the sugar until pale, then fold into the melted chocolate.
3. In a separate bowl, whip the egg whites with a pinch of salt until soft peaks form, gradually adding the remaining sugar.
4. In another bowl, whip the heavy cream with vanilla until soft peaks form.
5. Fold the whipped cream into the chocolate mixture.
6. Gently fold in the egg whites until no streaks remain.
7. Divide into serving cups and refrigerate for at least 3 hours before serving.

## Chocolate Brownies

### Description
Dense, fudgy chocolate bars with a crackly top, a classic baked treat.

### Ingredients
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/3 cup cocoa powder
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 cup chocolate chips (optional)

### Preparation
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line an 8x8-inch pan with parchment paper.
2. Melt the butter, then whisk in sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
3. Stir in cocoa powder, flour, salt, and baking powder until just combined.
4. Fold in chocolate chips if using.
5. Pour batter into the pan and spread evenly.
6. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs.
7. Cool completely before cutting into squares.

## Vanilla Waffles

### Description
Crisp on the outside, fluffy on the inside — classic breakfast waffles with a hint of vanilla.

### Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 1 3/4 cups milk
- 1/2 cup melted butter
- 2 tsp vanilla extract

### Preparation
1. Preheat the waffle iron and lightly grease it.
2. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
3. In another bowl, beat eggs, then add milk, melted butter, and vanilla.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until just combined.
5. Pour batter onto the waffle iron according to its size and cook until golden and crisp.
6. Serve warm with syrup, fruit, or whipped cream.

## Omelette

### Description
A quick, versatile egg dish that can be filled with cheese, vegetables, or meats.

### Ingredients
- 3 eggs
- 2 tbsp milk or water
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp butter
- Optional fillings: cheese, ham, spinach, mushrooms, onions

### Preparation
1. Whisk eggs with milk (or water), salt, and pepper until well combined.
2. Heat butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
3. Pour in the egg mixture and let it set slightly at the edges.
4. Add desired fillings to one half of the omelette.
5. Once mostly set, fold the omelette in half over the fillings.
6. Cook for another minute, then slide onto a plate and serve immediately.

## Classic Macaron with Cheese

### Description
A savory twist on the classic French macaron shell, filled with a creamy cheese filling instead of the traditional sweet ganache.

### Ingredients
- 100g almond flour
- 100g powdered sugar
- 90g egg whites (aged, at room temperature)
- 35g granulated sugar
- Pinch of salt
- For filling: 150g cream cheese, softened
- 50g grated parmesan
- 1 tbsp chives, finely chopped
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Preparation
1. Sift almond flour and powdered sugar together; set aside.
2. Whisk egg whites with a pinch of salt until foamy, then gradually add granulated sugar, beating to stiff, glossy peaks.
3. Fold the almond flour mixture into the meringue in batches until the batter flows like lava (macaronage).
4. Pipe small rounds onto a parchment-lined baking sheet and let rest for 30–45 minutes until a skin forms.
5. Bake at 300°F (150°C) for 15–18 minutes; let cool completely before removing from the sheet.
6. For the filling, mix cream cheese, parmesan, chives, salt, and pepper until smooth.
7. Pipe the cheese filling onto one shell and sandwich with another to form the macaron.

## Panna Cotta

### Description
A silky Italian dessert made by simmering cream and sugar, set with gelatin.

### Ingredients
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 tsp powdered gelatin
- 3 tbsp cold water
- Berries or fruit sauce for topping (optional)

### Preparation
1. Sprinkle gelatin over cold water in a small bowl and let it bloom for 5 minutes.
2. Heat cream and sugar in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring until sugar dissolves (do not boil).
3. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla extract and the bloomed gelatin until fully dissolved.
4. Pour the mixture into serving cups or ramekins.
5. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or until fully set.
6. Serve chilled, topped with berries or fruit sauce if desired.

## Lasagna

### Description
A hearty baked Italian dish with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese.

### Ingredients
- 12 lasagna noodles
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 24 oz marinara or tomato sauce
- 15 oz ricotta cheese
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- 1 egg
- Salt, pepper, and Italian herbs to taste

### Preparation
1. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions; drain and set aside.
2. In a pan, brown the ground beef with onion and garlic; drain excess fat.
3. Stir in the tomato sauce and simmer for 10 minutes; season with salt, pepper, and herbs.
4. In a bowl, mix ricotta, egg, and half of the parmesan.
5. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
6. In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella; repeat layers.
7. Top with remaining mozzarella and parmesan.
8. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes; uncover and bake for another 15–20 minutes until golden and bubbly.
9. Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing.

## Beignets

### Description
Light, pillowy French-style doughnuts, deep-fried and dusted generously with powdered sugar.

### Ingredients
- 1 cup warm water
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup evaporated milk
- 7 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup shortening
- Oil for frying
- Powdered sugar for dusting

### Preparation
1. Dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water; let sit for 5–10 minutes until foamy.
2. Add eggs and evaporated milk to the yeast mixture and stir to combine.
3. In a large bowl, mix flour and salt, then cut in the shortening.
4. Add the wet mixture to the dry ingredients and knead until a smooth dough forms.
5. Cover and refrigerate the dough for at least 4 hours (or overnight).
6. Roll the dough out to about 1/4-inch thickness and cut into squares.
7. Heat oil to 360°F (180°C) and fry the dough squares until golden on both sides.
8. Drain on paper towels and dust generously with powdered sugar while warm.

## Greek Salad

### Description
A refreshing, no-cook Mediterranean salad with crisp vegetables, olives, and feta cheese.

### Ingredients
- 4 tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 1 green bell pepper, sliced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup kalamata olives
- 200g feta cheese, cubed or sliced
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Preparation
1. Combine tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, and red onion in a large bowl.
2. Add the kalamata olives and toss gently.
3. Top with feta cheese.
4. Drizzle with olive oil and red wine vinegar.
5. Sprinkle with oregano, salt, and pepper.
6. Toss lightly just before serving to keep the feta intact.

## Handcrafted Home Bread

### Description
A simple, rustic homemade bread with a crisp crust and soft interior, made from scratch with basic pantry ingredients.

### Ingredients
- 4 cups flour
- 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast
- 1 1/2 cups warm water
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar

### Preparation
1. Dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water; let sit for 5–10 minutes until foamy.
2. In a large bowl, combine flour and salt, then make a well in the center.
3. Pour in the yeast mixture and olive oil; mix until a shaggy dough forms.
4. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
5. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm place for 1–1.5 hours, or until doubled in size.
6. Punch down the dough, shape it into a loaf, and place it on a baking sheet or in a loaf pan.
7. Cover and let rise again for 30–45 minutes.
8. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and bake for 25–30 minutes, until golden brown and hollow-sounding when tapped.
9. Cool on a wire rack before slicing.

## Blueberry Muffins

### Description
Soft, moist muffins bursting with fresh blueberries, perfect for breakfast or a snack.

### Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 + 1/2 cups fresh or frozen blueberries

### Preparation
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and line a muffin tin with paper liners.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
3. In another bowl, beat eggs, then add oil, milk, and vanilla; mix well.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
5. Gently fold in the blueberries, being careful not to overmix.
6. Divide the batter evenly among the muffin cups.
7. Bake for 18–22 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
8. Cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.

## Strawberry Milkshake

### Description
A creamy, fruity milkshake made with fresh strawberries, milk, and ice cream.

### Ingredients
- 1 cup fresh strawberries, hulled
- 1 cup milk
- 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
- 1 tbsp sugar (optional, to taste)
- Whipped cream for topping (optional)

### Preparation
1. Add strawberries, milk, ice cream, and sugar (if using) to a blender.
2. Blend on high speed until smooth and creamy.
3. Taste and adjust sweetness if needed.
4. Pour into a tall glass.
5. Top with whipped cream and a fresh strawberry, if desired, and serve immediately.

## Vanilla Milkshake

### Description
A classic, smooth milkshake with rich vanilla flavor, quick and easy to make.

### Ingredients
- 1 cup milk
- 3 scoops vanilla ice cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tbsp sugar (optional, to taste)
- Whipped cream for topping (optional)

### Preparation
1. Add milk, vanilla ice cream, vanilla extract, and sugar (if using) to a blender.
2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
3. Adjust thickness by adding more milk (thinner) or ice cream (thicker) as desired.
4. Pour into a glass.
5. Top with whipped cream if desired and serve immediately.
