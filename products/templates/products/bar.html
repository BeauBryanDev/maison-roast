The Twilight Hours

Welcome to the twilight hours at Maison-Roast. As the clock strikes 18:00, our space transforms, dimming the lights to unveil an exclusive, sophisticated vintage bar atmosphere. Out of deep respect for our younger patrons and family-friendly daytime crowd, our premium selection of artisanal beers, curated fine wines, and classic spirits is strictly reserved for the evening. Maison-Roast proudly stands as a smoking-friendly sanctuary; guests are warmly welcome to enjoy their cigarettes and cigars indoors, provided a peaceful, respectful, and orderly environment is maintained. Step inside, pour a glass, and let time slow down in our nostalgic retreat while you listen to our Reggae Singer.
